# firstmargin moderation policy

Last reviewed: 2026-09-08

## Policy direction

Moderation protects community participation while preserving fair process. Actions must be scoped, explainable, reversible where practical, and based on an identified policy violation rather than disagreement or popularity.

Staff choose the narrowest effective response based on documented facts. A report, criticism, repeated reporting, popularity, or disagreement alone does not establish a policy violation. Policy categories and changes must be documented.

## Member protections

- Affected people receive a clear explanation of an action and a path to appeal.
- Reporter identity and protected evidence remain confidential.
- Decisions and appeals must be reviewable; a decision maker cannot resolve their own appeal.
- Enforcement must not silently delete unrelated records or erase community history.

## Review and appeal principles

Moderation decisions remain reviewable and any correction preserves the decision history. The affected person can submit an appeal with an explanation or new evidence; a qualified independent reviewer resolves it. The public sees only the resulting availability, not report or evidence details.

## Implementation boundary

The current staff experience records reviews and recommendations. Destructive enforcement remains inactive until its full lifecycle is verified.
